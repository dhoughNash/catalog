How Back.Cat works

Back.Cat is your master song catalog. It keeps every song in one place with its writers, publishers and shares, tags each song so you can see which artists it suits, and adds up the royalty income each song has earned. This page walks you through it from the start.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and sign in with your email and password. It is the same account you use for Song Vault and Pitch Log.
  2. You land on the Catalog tab, which lists all your songs.
  3. Click any song to open its details on the right (on a phone, the details slide in over the list).
  4. Open the round account button at the top right and choose Help & how-to guide any time to come back to this page. Click Back to catalog when you are done.

Finding songs

  • Search box: type a title, writer, publisher, tag, a "sounds like" artist, or a work number.
  • All / Tagged / Untagged: handy for finding songs you have not tagged yet.
  • Status: show only Available, On hold, Cut or Released songs.
  • Sort: most income, title A–Z, or newest registered.
  • Filter by tags: opens a panel of tag buttons (see below).

Filtering by tags

Click Filter by tags, then click the tags you want. Picking several tags in the same group means "any of these". Picking tags from different groups means "all of these". For example, Female vocal plus Ballad or Slow tempo shows songs that are female-vocal and either ballad or slow. Your choices show as small buttons above the song list; click one to remove it, or click Clear.

Adding a song

  1. Click Add at the top right, then New song.
  2. Type the title, then fill in whatever you know. Only the title is needed to start.
  3. Add writers and publishers with their shares (see "The song panel").
  4. Click Add to catalog at the bottom of the panel.

Songs that come from co-writers in Song Vault are added from the Song Vault tab instead, which copies their writers, publishers and splits for you.

The song panel

Clicking a song opens a panel with everything about it. Change anything you like, then click Save changes at the bottom. A status next to the button tells you whether you have unsaved changes. Close the panel with the × button or the Esc key; if you have unsaved edits, you will be asked first.

What is in the panel

  • Total income: everything this song has earned across your imported statements. Click Statements inside the card to see which statements it came from.
  • Song details: status, your share of the song, BPM, key, alternate title, ISWC and a link to the demo.
  • Tags: the buttons that describe the song (more below).
  • Rights: the writers and publishers, each with a PRO (SESAC, BMI, ASCAP and so on) and a share. The line underneath adds them up. It should total 100%, and turns red if it does not. Work numbers from your PROs show here too.
  • Best fits: artists this song matches, once the song is tagged.
  • Notes: anything you want to remember.

Adding writers and publishers

Click Add writer or Add publisher, type the name, choose the PRO, and enter the share as a percent of the whole song. Use the × on the right to remove a row.

Deleting a song

Use the red Delete button at the bottom of a saved song. You will be asked to confirm.

Tags and best fits

Tags are how the app works out which artists a song might suit. In the song panel, click a tag to turn it on (it turns coral) and click it again to turn it off.

  • Vocal, Tempo feel, Demo level and Point of view: pick one.
  • Sub-genre, Topics and Mood: pick as many as fit.
  • + New: the dashed button at the end of a group adds a tag that is not on the list yet.
  • Sounds like: type artists or songs, separated by commas.
  • Sync-ready and I own the master: switch these to Yes when they are true.
The Best fits list needs at least a vocal, tempo, sub-genre or topic tag on the song. The more you tag, the better the matches. Untagged songs show a reminder instead.

Importing royalty statements

Importing a statement adds up what each song earned so the income numbers stay current. You can import a SESAC statement (CSV file) or a Me Gusta Music statement (PDF file).

  1. Click Add at the top right, then Import statement.
  2. Click the box to choose your file, or drag the file onto it.
  3. Read the preview. It shows the total, how many songs matched your catalog, and how many did not.
  4. Tick Add the unmatched songs to the catalog if you want songs that are not in your catalog yet created for you.
  5. Click the Import button, which shows the total amount.

Where the statement lets you choose who the income belongs to (for example Writer or Publisher), pick the right one in the preview before you import.

Things you might see

  • Already imported: if a statement was imported before, the preview says so. You can choose to replace the saved amount with the new file's amount, which is useful if a corrected statement arrives.
  • Account left out: if one account repeats the same lines as another account, it is skipped so income is not counted twice.
  • Different totals: if the total the app calculated differs from what the statement says, you will see a note. Check the file before importing.

Income is recorded exactly as the statement shows it. Any admin fee Me Gusta takes is not adjusted for.

View Statements

Open the account button and choose View Statements. This is the history of everything you have imported. Filter by Quarterly or Monthly, or by source. Each row has Rename (to change how the period is labelled) and Delete (which removes that statement's income from your songs, so use it with care).

Song Vault

Song Vault is where new co-writes from other writers arrive. This tab shows the ones that are not in your catalog yet.

  • Add as new song: copies the song, its writers, publishers and splits into your catalog.
  • Link to existing: appears when a song with the same title is already in your catalog. It connects the two instead of making a duplicate.
  • Add all: adds everything waiting in one click.

When nothing is waiting, the tab tells you everything is already in your catalog.

Tips and questions

My changes did not stick.

Edits are only kept after you click Save changes. The status beside the button shows when you have unsaved changes.

A song is missing from the list.

Check whether a search, status, tag filter or "Tagged/Untagged" choice is hiding it. The line under the Catalog title says how many songs are showing out of the total.

The income looks too low or too high.

Open the song and look at its statements list under Total income. If a statement was imported twice or is missing, fix it in View Statements (account button).

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. On a small screen the song panel opens over the list; use the × to go back.

How do I sign out?

Open the account button at the top right and choose Sign out.
